Run a spore round with the spore skill (`tools/spore.sh`, from a team folder `../../tools/spore.sh`):

1. Share: think back over what the team did since the last round (delivered PRs, fixed bugs). If a lesson could save another team time, announce it. If $ARGUMENTS is given, that is the lesson to share. Nothing worth sharing: say so in one line in `#spore`.
2. Take in: `pull`. Judge each spore yourself. Validate the ones that fit before you use them, integrate them into the team's own agent files, capabilities or genome with attribution, and record each one with `integrate <id> "<how>"`.

Tell me in three lines what you shared and what you integrated.
