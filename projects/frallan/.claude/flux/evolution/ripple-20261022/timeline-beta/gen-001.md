# Same-direction headway overlap

Compare delayed leaders and upcoming followers at a common station, activity
and explicit direction. Count distinct followers whose scheduled event is
reached by a leader's delayed event. Details proposed in CONTRACT.md.

Needs multiple trains' station announcements, stable IDs, event times, coverage
window, explicit direction and cancellation status. Latest-state aggregates
are insufficient. Route/track ambiguity prevents confirmed-conflict claims.

Advantage: explainable relationship between two trains, no historical database.
Limitation: advisory only; cannot determine signaling, parallel tracks or recovery.
Return insufficient data when required grouping is unavailable.

Evaluation cases: actual over estimate, Y boundary, opposite directions,
duplicate arrival/departure rows, multiple possible leaders, canceled/completed
followers, unknown direction, source freshness and coverage gaps.
